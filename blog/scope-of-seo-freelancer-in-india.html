    What to Do if You Want to Start a Career as an SEO Freelancer

    Starting a career as an SEO freelancer is a smart choice in 2026 because businesses of all sizes need online visibility to survive and grow. If you are planning to become an SEO freelancer, the first and most important step is learning the basics of SEO clearly. You should understand how search engines work, how websites rank on Google, and how users search for products or services online. Begin with fundamentals like keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, and off-page SEO. You can learn through a good digital marketing institute, online courses, blogs, and YouTube, but make sure your learning is practical, not just theoretical.

    The second step is practice. SEO cannot be learned only by reading or watching videos. Create your own website or blog and start working on it. Try to rank it for small keywords, optimize pages, improve content, and build backlinks. When you work on your own website, you understand real challenges like ranking delay, competition, and Google updates. This hands-on experience builds confidence and prepares you for client projects. Next, you should learn SEO tools that are commonly used in the industry. Tools like Google Search Console, Google Analytics, keyword research tools, and technical SEO tools are very important for an SEO freelancer. These tools help you analyze websites, track rankings, find errors, and measure results. Clients trust freelancers who can show data and performance reports, not just promises. After gaining basic knowledge and practice, start working with small clients. You can begin by helping local businesses, startups, or known contacts at a low budget. Focus on giving your best results instead of running after money in the beginning. Good results lead to testimonials, case studies, and referrals, which are very important for freelancing growth.

    You should also build your personal brand. Create a professional website, be active on LinkedIn, Instagram, or freelancing platforms, and share SEO tips or success stories. This helps people see you as an expert. Along with technical skills, work on your communication and client-handling skills, because clear communication helps in closing and retaining clients. Finally, keep yourself updated with the latest SEO trends. SEO changes frequently due to Google algorithm updates and AI-driven search. Continuous learning is the key to long-term success. If you stay consistent, honest, and result-oriented, you can build a stable and rewarding career as an SEO freelancer.

    What are techniques of doing SEO?

    There is various technique to optimize your website in search engine. On Page SEO
    Technical SEO
    Off Page SEO
    These all techniques are important while optimizing your website in search engine. On Page SEO is focused to optimize your website’s own pages using many On Page SEO techniques like keywords research, content optimization, headings, title, meta tags, alt tags, internal linking, outbound links, canonical tag, keywords placement in heading and sub headings and much more. Technical SEO relates with technical things of website, and an SEO executive must work on it before starting SEO for any website. It incudes URL structure, website structure, website responsiveness, website loading time, CSS optimization, JS Optimization, Image Size optimization, website navigation, sitemap, robots file etc. Off Page SEO is to work on making backlinks of your website, must differentiate between making backlinks and making quality backlinks. Creating numbers of backlinks without any consideration about the Domain Authority and Page Authority of the website where we are submitting our websites tending to get backlinks. Backlinks are also of two type while doing organic Off Page SEO. Do-Follow Backlinks and No-Follow Backlinks. We create both types of backlinks with ratio of 70% and 30%.
