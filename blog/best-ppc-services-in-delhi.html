What Are The Types of Bidding Strategies?

Google Ads offers 10 major bidding strategies tailored to your exact budget and campaign goals:

  • Target CPA (Cost Per Acquisition): Automatically sets bids to get as many conversions as possible at your set target cost per acquisition.
  • Target ROAS (Return On Ad Spend): Sets bids to maximize conversion value based on your target return on ad spend percentage.
  • CPM Bidding (Cost Per Thousand Impressions): Pay per 1,000 ad impressions on the Display Network.
  • vCPM Bidding (Cost Per Viewable Impression): Pay only for impressions that are measured as viewable by real users.
  • CPV Bidding (Cost Per View): Pay per video view or engagement on YouTube campaigns.
  • Maximize Conversions: Automatically spends your budget to capture the maximum number of conversions.
  • Enhanced Cost Per Click (ECPC): Adjusts manual bids to raise conversions while maintaining target cost per conversion.
  • Maximize Clicks: Automatically sets bids to help get as many clicks as possible within your budget.
  • Manual CPC Bidding: Gives you total manual control over setting maximum cost per click bids.
  • Target Impression Share Bidding: Sets bids to show your ad at the top of the Google search page.
